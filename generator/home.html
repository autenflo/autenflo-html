Kya mujhe naya staff ya agency rakhni padegi?

Aksar nahi. AI roz ke manual aur repetitive kaam sambhalta hai: entry, follow-up, reminder, billing, reports aur content. Wo kisi insaan ka poora kaam nahi leta, par jo kaam aap naya hire karke karwate, uska bada hissa bina naye staff ke ho jata hai. Free audit me hum saaf bata dete hain ki aapko kitna chahiye.

Kaun-kaun se processes automate ho sakte hain?

Jo kaam repetitive hai, rules par chalta hai aur roz dohrata hai: enquiry handling, follow-ups, data entry, quotation aur invoice, payment reminders, reports, customer support aur marketing content. Free audit me hum aapke business ke hisaab se list banate hain.

Kya mujhe technical hona padega?

Bilkul nahi. Aap bas batao ki kaam kaise chalta hai. Setup, testing aur dekhbhal hamari zimmedari hai.

Mere maujooda software ya register badalne padenge?

Nahi. Hum aapke maujooda systems ke saath hi kaam karte hain aur unhe aapas me jodte hain. Jo kaam abhi register ya diary me hota hai, use bhi digital system me le aate hain.

Customer ko pata chal jayega ki ye AI hai?

Haan. Pehle message me saaf likha hota hai ki ye AI sahayak hai, aur insaan se baat maangte hi chat seedha aapko ya aapke staff ko chali jaati hai. Clinic aur school jaise jagah par ye aur bhi zaroori hai.

AI galat kaam kar de to?

Isiliye hum pehle demo dikhate hain, jahan zaroori ho wahan insaan ki approval rakhte hain, aur live hone ke baad bhi kaam check karte rehte hain. Risky ya zaroori cheezein AI khud rok kar aapko bhej deta hai.

Pehla process aam taur par 2 se 4 hafte me live ho jata hai.
