Terms

Terms: kya milta hai, kya nahi, aur band kaise karein

Service, charges, AI ki seema aur band karne ke niyam.

Antim update: 12 November 2026

Hamari service

autenflo AI se business ke repetitive processes (reply, follow-up, entry, reminder, reports aur sambandhit kaam) sambhalta hai, jaisa aapke plan ya agreement me likha ho. Naye kaam ya add-on alag se tay hote hain.

Charges

Ek baar ka installation aur monthly charge plan ke hisaab se. Messaging platform aur API ke charges, AI usage aur domain jaise third-party kharche aapke apne account se jaate hain aur hamare charge me shamil nahi hain.

AI aur uski seema

AI kabhi-kabhi galat reply de sakta hai. Hum galat jawab ke rules aur insaan tak handover set karte hain aur replies par nazar rakhte hain, par poori guarantee nahi de sakte. Customer ko AI sahayak hone ki baat saaf dikhaai jaati hai.

Aapki zimmedari

Aap sahi jaankari (rate, offer, FAQ) dete hain, aur sirf un logon ko message bhejte hain jinhone sampark kiya ho ya jinki anumati ho. Platform ke niyam todne par account ban ho sakta hai, jiske liye hum zimmedar nahi honge.

Data

Aapke business aur customers ka data aapka hai. Hum use sirf service dene ke liye use karte hain.

Band karna

Koi lamba contract nahi. Aap [30 din ke notice] par service band kar sakte hain. Us mahine tak ka monthly charge lagta hai. [Refund ka niyam yahan likhein.]

Seema aur kanoon

Hamari zimmedari us mahine ke monthly charge tak seemit rahegi. Ye shartein Bharat ke kanoon ke adheen hain. [Vivad ke liye adhikar-kshetra: Muzaffarpur, Bihar.]
