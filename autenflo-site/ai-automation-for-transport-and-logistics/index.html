Transport & Logistics

Transport aur logistics ke liye AI jo "gaadi kahan hai" ke har call ka jawab khud deta hai.

Party ne subah poochha "Patna se Ranchi 9 ton ka rate?", dopahar ko "gaadi kahan hai?", aur shaam ko "bilty bhejo". Aap driver se baat kar rahe ho aur teeno ka jawab pending hai. Jo transporter pehle rate aur status batata hai, party wahi se maal bhejti hai.

Transport & Logistics me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Transport ka business teen cheezon par chalta hai: gaadi ko load kitni jaldi mila, party ko status time par mila, aur freight ka payment time par aaya. Teeno jagah kaam phone, diary aur WhatsApp me bikhra hota hai. Cab, travels aur tour operators ke liye season me yehi bheed aur badh jati hai. Neeche wahi 6 jagah hain jahan time aur paisa chhootta hai.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Rate aur gaadi ki availability ke har call ka manual jawab

Problem: Party poochhti hai "9 ton, Patna se Ranchi, kal loading?". Aap gaadi ya driver ke saath busy ho, jawab late gaya, aur party ne dusre transporter ko maal de diya.

autenflo: Route ke hisaab se rate card se turant quote jata hai, gaadi ki availability batayi jati hai aur serious party ka alert seedha aapko aata hai.

02

"Gaadi kahan hai?" ke baar-baar calls

Problem: Har party din me kai baar poochhti hai. Aapko driver ko call karna padta hai, phir party ko. Ek trip par 5-6 calls ka time jata hai.

autenflo: Loading, raste me aur delivery ka status party ko WhatsApp par apne aap milta hai. Party ko jawab ke liye aapko call nahi karna padta.

03

Bilty, LR aur challan ki entry register me

Problem: Har trip ki bilty aur challan ki entry haath se hoti hai. POD ki photo kisi phone me padi rehti hai aur bill banate waqt dhoondhni padti hai.

autenflo: Trip ki details se bilty ka data auto entry hota hai, POD ki photo aate hi record me jud jati hai, aur party ko bhi chali jati hai.

04

Freight ka payment aur party-wise baaki ginna

Problem: Kis party ka kitna baaki hai, ye ginte-ginte din nikal jata hai. Maal pahunch gaya par payment ke liye ek-ek ko call karna padta hai.

autenflo: POD ke baad bill auto jata hai, due date se pehle tameez ka reminder jata hai, aur roz shaam ko party-wise outstanding ka summary aapko milta hai.

05

Driver ko trip ki details ek-ek karke batana

Problem: Loading ka address, party ka number, maal ki details: sab driver ko phone par batani padti hain. Kaun si gaadi khali hai, ye yaad rakhna padta hai.

autenflo: Trip ki details driver ko WhatsApp par auto jati hain, khali gaadiyon ki list ek jagah rehti hai, aur staff ko kaam auto assign hota hai.

06

Season me cab aur tour booking ki bheed

Problem: Pitru Paksha, Shravani Mela ya shaadi ke season me cab aur tour ki enquiry ek saath aati hai. Confirmation, driver ki detail aur pickup ka time phone par bikhra rehta hai.

autenflo: Rate aur availability ka turant jawab, booking WhatsApp par confirm, yatri aur driver dono ko pickup reminder, aur trip ke baad feedback auto.

Mann me jo sawal hain

Transport & Logistics: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Transport business ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye rate aur gaadi ki availability turant batata hai, trip ka status party ko bhejta hai, bilty aur challan ka record rakhta hai, aur freight ka payment reminder deta hai. Sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par chalta hai.

Kya chhote transporter ya 4-5 gaadi wale ke liye bhi chalega?

Haan. Chhote transporter par sab kaam owner khud karta hai, isliye calls aur diary me sabse zyada time jata hai. Automation yehi repetitive kaam sambhal leta hai.

Kya ye GPS ya tracking system ke saath chalega?

Agar aapke paas GPS ya tracking ka system hai to aksar usse jodna ho sakta hai. Agar nahi hai, to driver ke update se bhi status party tak jata hai. Free audit me hum aapka setup dekhkar batate hain.

Party ko "gaadi kahan hai" ka jawab AI kaise dega?

Driver ya aap trip ka status update karte ho, aur AI wahi jaankari party ko WhatsApp par bhejta hai. Jo jaankari AI ke paas nahi hoti, wo wahi se aap tak pahunch jati hai.

Bilty aur LR ki entry bhi hogi?

Trip ki details se bilty aur challan ka data auto entry ho jata hai. Aapke maujooda format aur software ke saath kaise judega, ye pehle process dekhkar tay hota hai.

Freight ka payment kaise vasool hoga?

POD aate hi bill party ko chala jata hai, due date se pehle tameez ka reminder jata hai, aur payment aate hi status update ho jata hai. Aap tay karte ho kitni baar reminder jaye.

Cab, travels aur tour operators ke liye bhi hai?

Haan. Rate aur availability ka turant jawab, booking confirmation, driver ki detail aur pickup reminder auto jate hain. Season ki bheed me bhi ek bhi enquiry nahi chhootti.

Kya driver ko bhi kuch seekhna padega?

Driver ko bas WhatsApp par message dekhna aur status bhejna hota hai. Koi alag app ya technical kaam nahi hai.

Party ka data kaise rakha jata hai?

Party ka naam, number aur trip ki details aapke apne account me rehti hain aur sirf aapke kaam ke liye use hoti hain. Hum privacy policy me likhe niyam follow karte hain.

Shuruaat kitne se hoti hai?
