Hum kaun hain

autenflo Muzaffarpur, Bihar se chalta hai. Hum chhote aur beech ke business ke liye AI se manual aur repetitive processes (data entry, follow-up, billing, reminders, reports aur marketing) automate karte hain, aur Hindi me, aapke saath baith kar setup karte hain.

Hum kaise kaam karte hain

  • Pehle free process audit: aapke manual aur repetitive kaam gin kar batate hain ki kitna time aur paisa ja raha hai.

  • Saaf kharcha: hamari fees, platform aur API ka kharcha, sab pehle likh kar dete hain.
  • Likhit agreement: kaam ka scope, data kiska hai, aur band kaise karna hai.
