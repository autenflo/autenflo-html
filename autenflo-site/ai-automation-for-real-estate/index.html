Real Estate: Dealers, Builders aur Colonizers

Real estate me AI jo har lead ko site visit tak le jata hai.

Ek plot ki enquiry Facebook se aati hai, doosri 99acres se, teesri kisi dalal ke phone se. Teeno alag jagah padi rehti hain aur jis lead ko do din jawab nahi mila, wo kisi aur ke saath site dekh chuka hota hai.

Ye page kis ke liye hai

Real Estate: Dealers, Builders aur Colonizers me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Property ka sauda mahino chalta hai, isliye yahan sabse bada nuksan bhoolne se hota hai: kis ko site dikhani thi, kis se booking ka paisa aana hai, kaun sirf pooch raha hai aur kaun kharidne ko tayyar hai. Neeche wahi 6 jagah hain jahan dealer, builder aur colonizer ka time aur sauda dono jaate hain.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Alag-alag sources ki leads ek jagah nahi

Problem: Facebook ad, property portal, board par likha number aur reference, sab ke call alag phone par aate hain. Month ke end me kisi ko nahi pata kaun sa source chala.

autenflo: Har source ki lead ek hi sheet me naam, budget, area aur source ke saath apne aap aati hai, taaki pata chale paisa kahan kaam kar raha hai.

02

Site visit ka follow-up chhoot jata hai

Problem: Visit ke baad customer ne "sochte hain" kaha aur baat wahin khatam. Agla call kisne karna tha, kisi ko yaad nahi.

autenflo: Visit se pehle reminder aur location pin, aur visit ke baad 1, 3 aur 7 din par alag message. Jawab aate hi sales wale ko alert.

03

Brochure, rate aur details baar-baar bhejna

Problem: Har customer ko wahi brochure, wahi rate list aur wahi map bhejna padta hai, din me 20 baar.

autenflo: Customer ke poochte hi project ka brochure, plot size, rate aur location turant chala jata hai.

04

Kaun hot lead hai, pata hi nahi

Problem: Jo customer 3 baar budget aur registry ka timeline pooch chuka hai wo aur jo sirf rate dekh kar chala gaya, dono ek barabar queue me hain.

autenflo: AI budget, timeline aur sawalon se hot lead pehchanta hai aur sirf unka alert seedha aapko deta hai.

05

Booking payment aur installment due ka hisaab

Problem: Kisne token diya, kis ki kist is mahine aani hai, kis ki pichhle mahine thi, ye sab diary aur memory me chalta hai.

autenflo: Payment schedule ek jagah rehta hai aur due se pehle customer ko tameez ka reminder jata hai. Payment aate hi status badal jata hai.

06

Team ki activity ka report manually banana

Problem: Kis agent ne kitne call kiye, kitne visit karwaye, kitne band hue, ye jaanne ke liye roz sabko puchna padta hai.

autenflo: Roz shaam ko team ki calls, visits aur bookings ka summary owner ko WhatsApp par aa jata hai.

Real Estate: Dealers, Builders aur Colonizers: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Real estate ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye alag-alag sources ki leads ek jagah laata hai, brochure aur rate turant bhejta hai, site visit book karta hai aur visit ke baad follow-up karta hai. Hot lead ka alert seedha aapko milta hai.

Kya chhote property dealer ke liye bhi ye theek hai?

Haan. Jo dealer akela 15 se 20 leads sambhalta hai, wahi sabse zyada leads bhoolta hai. Automation uska follow-up ka kaam sambhal leta hai.

AI hot lead kaise pehchanta hai?

Wo customer ke sawal, budget, timeline aur reply ki speed dekhta hai. Aap batate ho kaun si baat serious interest dikhati hai, aur usi ke hisaab se alert aata hai.

Kya brochure aur rate list customer ko khud bhejega?

Haan. Customer ke project ya plot size poochte hi sahi brochure, rate aur location bhej diya jata hai. Rate badalne par ek baar update karna hota hai.

Site visit ka reminder kaise kaam karta hai?

Visit se ek din pehle aur kuch ghante pehle customer ko time aur location pin jata hai. Customer ek tap me time badal bhi sakta hai aur aapko turant pata chalta hai.

Kya installment aur booking payment ke reminder bhi jayenge?

Haan. Payment schedule daalne ke baad due date se pehle tameez wala reminder jata hai. Payment aate hi reminder band ho jata hai.

Kya ye 99acres ya Facebook leads ke saath chalega?

Aksar haan. Jo lead WhatsApp ya form se aati hai wo seedha judti hai. Portal ke hisaab se jodne ka tareeka hum setup ke waqt dekhte hain.

Kya customer ko lagega ki robot se baat ho rahi hai?

Jawab Hindi me, aapke andaaz me aur seedhe hote hain. Jab customer ko insaan se baat karni ho, to baat aapke ya aapke agent ke paas chali jati hai.

Team ke har agent ka hisaab kaise milta hai?

Roz shaam ko summary aati hai ki kis agent ne kitni leads li, kitne visit karwaye aur kitne band hue. Alag se kisi se poochna nahi padta.
