Coaching Institute Ke Liye WhatsApp Automation: Admission Se Fees Tak

Coaching aur tuition ke liye WhatsApp automation: enquiry, demo class, admission aur fees ke 5 padav, har padav ka message aur kya automate na karein.

Seedha jawab

Coaching me WhatsApp automation 5 padav sambhalta hai: enquiry ka reply, demo class booking, admission follow-up, fees reminder aur parent update. Jo padav sabse zyada chhoota hai wo enquiry ke baad ka follow-up hai, isliye wahin se shuru kijiye.

Coaching ka business ek seedhi line me chalta hai: enquiry, demo, admission, fees, aur phir result. Is line me jahan bhi tootna hota hai wahan ek student ya ek parent chhoot jata hai. WhatsApp par iska poora kaam chal sakta hai, bas har padav ka alag message aur alag niyam chahiye. Is article me ek-ek padav ko chhota karke bataya hai.

Padav 1: Enquiry ka reply

Parent aksar raat ko poochhta hai, jab kaam se wapas aata hai ya bachche ko padhate hue socha. Aur wo 2-3 coaching se ek saath poochhta hai. Jo pehle sahi jawab de deta hai, parent usi se baat aage badhata hai. Is padav par kaam: turant greeting, class, batch timing, fees ka range (ya fees ka tareeka), aur demo class ka offer. Message templates ke liye alag article hai: coaching me admission enquiry ka reply kaise karein.

Padav 2: Demo class booking

Demo class wo jagah hai jahan parent ka bharosa banta hai. Yahan system ye karta hai:

  • Parent ko 2-3 demo ke slot batata hai.
  • Parent ka chunaa hua slot save karta hai.
  • Ek din pehle aur 2 ghante pehle reminder bhejta hai.
  • Demo ke baad "kaisa laga?" ka ek chhota message bhejta hai.

Demo ka reminder bahut zaroori hai kyunki bahut se parent bina batae nahi aate. Isse aapka teacher ka time bachta hai.

Padav 3: Admission ka follow-up

Demo ke baad aksar parent "sochke bataate hain" kehta hai. Yahan sabse zyada admission chhootte hain. Is padav par teen follow-up hone chahiye: 1 din, 3 din aur 7 din ke baad. Har message me ek nayi cheez ho: pehle me demo ka feedback, doosre me batch me bachi jagah, teesre me seedha aur izzat wala "agar abhi sahi samay nahi to koi baat nahi". Ye kaise likhna hai, iske liye follow-up kab karein padhiye.

Padav 4: Fees ka collection

Fees reminder sabse awkward kaam hai. Isliye isko system ko dena sabse achha hai, kyunki system naram aur barabar bhasha me kehta hai. Kuch niyam:

  • Due date se 3 din pehle ek yaad dilane wala message.
  • Due date ke din ek message jisme payment ka tareeka ho (UPI link ya account).
  • Due date ke 3 din baad ek naram message, aur uske baad insaan call kare.
  • Kabhi bachche ke saamne ya bachche ke number par fees ka message na bhejiye. Parent ke number par hi bhejiye.

Padav 5: Parent update

Attendance, test ka result, chhuttiyaan, nayi batch ki jaankari. Ye sab ek jagah se parent tak jaane chahiye. Iska fayda: parent ka bharosa badhta hai aur aapko baar baar call nahi aate. Dhyan rahe ki bachche ki privacy aur result sirf us ke parent ko jaaye.

Kya automate na karein

  • Scholarship ya fees me chhoot ka faisla. Ye aapka faisla hai, system ka nahi.
  • Parent ki shikayat. Yahan seedha insaan baat kare.
  • Bachche ki padhai ki kamzori. Teacher batayein, message nahi.

Shuru kaise karein

  1. Apne pichhle mahine ki enquiry gin lijiye: kitni aayi, kitni demo tak pahunchi, kitni ne admission li.
  2. Jahan sabse zyada gir rahe hain, wahi padav pehle chuniye.
  3. Us padav ke message likhiye aur 2 hafte chalaiye.
  4. Phir number dekhkar agla padav jodiye.

Do aur zaroori templates

Demo ka reminder

Namaste [parent ka naam] ji, [bachche ka naam] ki demo class kal [time] par [coaching ka naam] me hai. Address: [map link]. Kisi karan se na aa paayein to "2" likhiye, hum naya samay de denge.

Fees ka naram reminder

Namaste [parent ka naam] ji, [bachche ka naam] ki [mahina] ki fees ₹[amount] [date] ko due hai. Payment ke liye UPI: [id]. Bhar dene ke baad screenshot yahin bhej dijiye, hum receipt bhej denge. Koi dikkat ho to batayein.

Teen number jo har mahine dekhiye

  1. Enquiry se demo: 100 enquiry me se kitne demo tak aaye.
  2. Demo se admission: demo dekhne wale kitne admission tak pahunche.
  3. Fees time par: kitne parent due date ke andar fees dete hain.

Teeno ko ek notebook ya sheet me likhiye. Jahan sabse bada girna dikhe, wahin sudhaar ka pehla kaam hai. Bina number ke koi bhi automation andhere me teer chalane jaisa hai.

Ye galtiyan coaching wale aksar karte hain

  • Sab batches ko ek hi message. Class 10 ke parent ko NEET ke batch ki jaankari bhejna.
  • Fees ka message group me. Fees ya result ki baat kabhi bhi group me na jaye. Hamesha parent ke private chat me.
  • Demo ke baad chup ho jana. Zyadatar admission yahin chhootte hain, enquiry ke samay nahi.
  • Staff ko ye na batana ki kaun sa kaam system karega. Isse reply aur follow-up dono dohre jaate hain.

Inme se koi bhi cheez kisi tool ki kami nahi hai, ye process ki kami hai. Pehle process saaf kijiye, phir system.

Meri raay

Coaching me sabse bada nuksaan "demo ke baad ki chuppi" me hota hai. Agar aap sirf ek kaam abhi shuru kar sakte hain, to demo ke baad ka follow-up shuru kijiye. Baaki sab uske baad.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Coaching me WhatsApp automation kaise shuru karein?

Pehle apne enquiry se admission tak ke numbers dekhiye. Jahan sabse zyada log chhoot rahe hain wahin ka message likhiye, jaise demo ke baad ka follow-up, aur 2 hafte chalakar dekhiye.

Kya fees ka reminder parent ko bura lagta hai?

Agar message naram ho, due date se pehle aaye aur parent ke number par jaye to aam taur par theek rehta hai. Bachche ke number par fees ka message na bhejiye.

Kya demo class ka reminder zaroori hai?

Haan. Bahut se parent bina batae demo me nahi aate, aur ek reminder se teacher ka time bachta hai aur demo ki attendance behtar hoti hai.
