15 minute me pata karein kitni enquiry miss hui

15 minute me apne WhatsApp ki enquiries gin kar dekhiye kitni late ya miss hui, aur us ka lagbhag rupaye me hisaab.

Zyadatar owner ko lagta hai ki enquiry thodi hi miss hoti hai. Gin kar dekhne par aksar number badha nikalta hai. Ye check aap khud 15 minute me kar sakte hain.

Kadam 1: pichhle 7 din ki list banaiye

WhatsApp, Instagram aur call log se har enquiry ek sheet me likhiye: kab aayi, kisne bheji, kab reply gaya.

Kadam 2: teen cheezein ginein

  1. Kul kitni enquiry aayi.
  2. Kitni ka reply 1 ghante ke baad gaya, ya gaya hi nahi.
  3. Kitni ka follow-up kabhi hua hi nahi.

Kadam 3: rupaye me badliye

Late ya miss enquiry ko, jitne % customer theek time par reply milne par ban jaate hain, us se multiply kijiye, phir ek customer ki average value se.

Late enquiry x conversion % x ek customer ki value = lagbhag itne ka mauka gaya.

Ye andaza hai, guarantee nahi. Par ye aapka apna data hai, aur isse saaf ho jata hai ki reply ki speed par kaam karna chahiye ya nahi.
