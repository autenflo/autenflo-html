Follow-up Kab Karein: Customer Ko Pareshan Kiye Bina Message Kaise Bhejein

Customer follow-up kab karein aur kitni baar? 1-3-7 din ka rule, business ke hisaab se timing, kab rukna hai aur pareshan kiye bina kaise yaad dilayein.

Seedha jawab

Zyada tar business me 1, 3 aur 7 din par follow-up kaafi hota hai. Har message me ek nayi kaam ki baat ho, samay kaam ke ghanton me ho, aur jo customer mana kar de use dobara message na jaye. Property aur B2B me gap lamba hota hai, coaching aur clinic me chhota.

Follow-up me sabse bada darr hota hai: "customer ko bura na lage". Aur isi darr se aadhe business follow-up karte hi nahi, aur baaki aadhe itna karte hain ki customer block kar deta hai. Sach ye hai ki customer ko follow-up se nahi, bekaar follow-up se pareshani hoti hai. Agar aapka message kaam ka hai, sahi samay par hai aur izzat se likha hai, to zyada tar customer use pasand karte hain. Is article me seedha bataya hai ki kab, kitni baar aur kaise.

Pehle ye samajhiye ki customer follow-up kyun chahta hai

Customer ne poochha, aapne jawab diya, aur phir wo kaam me laga gaya. Kuch hi ghanton me wo bhool jata hai ki usne kya poochha tha. Ya wo "baad me dekhunga" soch kar chhod deta hai. Aapka follow-up use yaad dilata hai aur kabhi kabhi usko wo madad deta hai jo wo maangna bhool gaya. Isliye follow-up "peechha karna" nahi, "madad ka dobara hath badhana" hai.

1-3-7 din ka rule

DinMessage ka kaamKya likhein
Din 1Yaad dilana"Kal aapne [cheez] poochhi thi, kuch aur jaankari chahiye?"
Din 3Kaam ki nayi cheezRate, photo, slot ya offer jo pehle nahi bataya
Din 7Izzat se aakhri message"Abhi sahi samay nahi to koi baat nahi, jab chahein message kar dijiye"

Isme har message ka kaam alag hai. Din 1 me aap sirf yaad dilate hain, Din 3 me kuch naya dete hain, aur Din 7 me jaane dete hain. Ye teen kadam customer ko dabaav nahi, jagah dete hain.

Business ke hisaab se timing alag

BusinessFollow-up ka gapWajah
Dukaan / retailSame din ya agle din, phir 3 dinFaisla jaldi hota hai
Coaching1, 3, 7 din (demo ke baad)Parent parivaar se baat karta hai
ClinicAppointment ke baad 1 din; follow-up visit ki date parDoctor ke bataye samay par
Property1, 3, 7, 15, 30 dinFaisla hafton-mahino me hota hai
Wholesale / B2B2-3 din ka gap, mahine me ek baar regularOrder ka chakra lamba hota hai

Din ka kaunsa samay

Aam taur par subah 10 se dopahar 1 aur shaam 5 se 8 baje ke beech ke message zyada dekhe jaate hain. Raat 10 ke baad aur subah 7 se pehle ka message pareshan karta hai, chahe kitna bhi zaroori ho. Sunday ya tyohaar par bina zaroori kaam ke message mat bhejiye. Ye thumb rule hai, aap apne customer ka behavior dekhkar badal sakte hain.

Kab follow-up band karein

  1. Customer saaf mana kar de. "Nahi chahiye" ka matlab nahi chahiye.
  2. Teen follow-up ho gaye aur koi jawab nahi. Aur zyada bhejna pareshan karta hai.
  3. Customer kehde "message mat bhejiye". Use list se turant hata dijiye.
  4. Customer ne khareed liya. Usko ab purani enquiry ka follow-up nahi, naye order ki aabhaar wali baat bhejiye.

Pareshan na karne ke 6 tareeke

  • Har baar kuch naya dijiye. Wahi sawal dobara mat poochhiye.
  • Chhota likhiye. 3-4 line.
  • Jawab dena aasan banaiye. "Haan ya na" ya "1 ya 2" jaise sawal.
  • Naam se baat kijiye. "Namaste Rakesh ji."
  • Dabaav na daaliye. "Aaj hi karna padega" jaisa jhootha pressure customer turant pehchaan leta hai.
  • Opt-out ka rasta rakhiye. "Aap message nahi chahte to bata dijiye" ek line me.

Kya follow-up bina permission ke bhej sakte hain

Seedha rule: sirf unhe bhejiye jinhone khud aapse baat ki ho ya jinse aapne permission li ho. Kisi ka number kisi list se uthakar bulk me promotion bhejna galat bhi hai aur WhatsApp par aapke number ke restrict hone ka khatra bhi. Follow-up un logo ke liye hai jinhone pehle poochha hai, na ki kisi bhi anjaan ke liye.

Follow-up ko track kaise karein

Ek simple sheet banaiye jisme ye columns hon: customer ka naam, number, kya poochha, kab poochha, kitne follow-up gaye, aur agla follow-up kab. Roz shaam 15 minute ye sheet kholiye aur us din ke sabhi follow-up bhej dijiye. Jaise hi koi customer jawab de, uski row ka status badal dijiye. Is ek sheet se aapke 90% follow-up sambhal jaate hain.

Jab follow-up haath se mushkil ho jaye

Jab roz 15-20 se zyada enquiry aati hai, to har ek ka alag follow-up yaad rakhna mushkil ho jata hai. Tab system har enquiry ko 1, 3 aur 7 din par apne aap message bhejta hai, aur jo jawab de de use rok kar aapke paas bhejta hai. Templates ke liye follow-up message template Hindi me dekhiye. Property ke liye alag flow yahan likha hai. Aur plans me dekhiye ki hum kya sambhalte hain.

Follow-up ke 3 asli udaharan: kaisa kaam karta hai

Dukaan: Customer ne sofa ka rate poochha aur chala gaya. Agle din: "Kal aapne sofa ka rate poochha tha, 3 naye design aaye hain, photo bhejun?" Ye message nayi cheez deta hai, sirf yaad nahi dilata.

Coaching: Demo ke baad parent chup hai. 3 din baad: "Class 9 ke batch me is hafte 4 seat bachi hain. Koi sawal ho to batayein." Ye sach hona chahiye, warna mat likhiye.

Clinic: Patient ka follow-up visit due hai. "Doctor ne [din] ko dobara dikhane ko kaha tha. Kal [time] ka slot khali hai, book kar dun?" Isme dabaav nahi, madad hai.

Teeno me ek cheez common hai: pichhli baat ka hawala, ek nayi cheez, aur ek seedha sawal.

Meri raay

Follow-up ka darr customer se nahi, apne se aata hai. Agar aapka message madad karta hai, to customer ko bura nahi lagta. Ek rule yaad rakhiye: har message me ek nayi, kaam ki baat aur ek saaf sawal. Isse zyada ki zaroorat nahi, aur isse kam me follow-up bekaar hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Customer ko follow-up kitne din baad karna chahiye?

Aam taur par 1, 3 aur 7 din baad. Property me gap lamba hota hai (1, 3, 7, 15, 30 din), jabki dukaan me same din ya agle din se shuru kar sakte hain.

Kitni baar follow-up karna theek hai?

Aam taur par 3 baar kaafi hai. Uske baad bhi jawab na aaye to rukiye. Jo customer mana kar de use dobara message mat bhejiye.

Kya follow-up ke liye customer ki permission chahiye?

Behtar hai. Sirf unhe bhejiye jinhone khud aapse baat ki ho. Anjaan logo ko bulk me message bhejna galat hai aur aapke WhatsApp number ke restrict hone ka khatra badhata hai.
