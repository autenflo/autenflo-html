Clinic Ke Liye WhatsApp Appointment Reminder: Setup Guide

Clinic me WhatsApp appointment reminder kaise lagayein: kab bhejein, kya likhein, consent, Business app vs API aur patient ki privacy ke niyam.

Seedha jawab

Clinic me appointment reminder do baar bhejiye: ek din pehle aur 2-3 ghante pehle. Message me naam, time, doctor aur address ho, aur reschedule ka aasan rasta ho. Medical details message me na likhiye, aur patient ki sahmati pehle le lijiye.

Clinic me ek khali slot ka matlab hai ek doctor ka ghanta bina kaam ke. Aur jab patient bina batae nahi aata, to dusre patient ko wo slot mil sakta tha. WhatsApp reminder is nuksaan ko kam karne ka sabse sasta tareeka hai. Par ye kaam tabhi karta hai jab sahi tareeke se set ho. Is guide me seedha bataya hai kaise.

Reminder me kya hona chahiye

Ek achha appointment reminder chhota hota hai aur usme 5 cheezein hoti hain:

  1. Patient ka naam
  2. Doctor ka naam aur clinic ka naam
  3. Din, date aur time
  4. Clinic ka address aur map link
  5. Confirm ya reschedule karne ka aasan tareeka

Isme beemari ya ilaaj ki baat nahi likhni chahiye. Ye jaankari private hoti hai aur galat number par jaa sakti hai.

Kab bhejein: do reminder ka niyam

ReminderSamayKaam
PehlaEk din pehle, shaam 5-7 bajePatient ko yaad dilana, confirm karwana
DoosraAppointment se 2-3 ghante pehleAakhri yaad, map link
Teesra (optional)Visit ke baad, 1 din meDhanyavaad aur follow-up ki jaankari

Reminder ke 3 templates

1. Ek din pehle

Namaste [naam] ji, [clinic ka naam] me aapka appointment kal [din] ko [time] par [doctor ka naam] ke saath hai. Address: [address / map link]. Confirm karne ke liye "1" aur samay badalne ke liye "2" likhiye.

2. Do-teen ghante pehle

Namaste [naam] ji, aaj [time] par [clinic ka naam] me aapka appointment hai. Kripya 10 minute pehle pahunch jaayein. Map: [link].

3. Visit ke baad

Namaste [naam] ji, aaj aane ke liye dhanyavaad. Agar doctor ne diya hua koi sawal ho to yahin likh dijiye. Agla appointment [date] ko hai, hum yaad dila denge.

Setup kaise karein: do raaste

Raasta 1: WhatsApp Business app (chhote clinic ke liye)

Agar roz 10-15 appointment hote hain, to Business app me labels aur quick replies se kaam chal sakta hai. Receptionist shaam ko kal ki list dekhkar manually bhejti hai. Isme kharcha nahi hota, par receptionist ka roz 20-30 minute lagta hai, aur kisi din bhool jaye to reminder nahi jata.

Raasta 2: API aur automation (zyada appointment ke liye)

Jab appointment zyada hon ya kai doctor hon, tab system appointment list se apne aap reminder bhejta hai. Isme reminder ke liye template message Meta se pehle approve karwana padta hai. Aur WhatsApp par template message ka Meta ka charge category ke hisaab se lagta hai. Comparison ke liye Business app vs API padhiye.

Consent aur privacy: ye mat bhooliye

  • Patient se pehle se permission lijiye. Registration form ya reception par ek line: "Kya hum appointment reminder WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain?"
  • Sirf zaroori jaankari. Naam, time, doctor. Diagnosis ya report ki baat message me nahi.
  • Galat number ka dhyan. Number save karte waqt dohrakar check kijiye.
  • Opt-out. Jo patient message nahi chahta, use list se hata dijiye.
  • Data ka dhyan. Patient ka data sirf clinic ke kaam ke liye rakhiye. Data protection ke niyam ke baare me apne vakeel ya CA se ek baar confirm kar lijiye.

Reschedule ko aasan banaiye

Zyada tar patient isliye nahi aate kyunki wo samay badalna chahte hain par kaise karein ye nahi pata. Isliye reminder me hi reschedule ka aasan tareeka dijiye: "2 likhiye" ya ek call number. Phir wo slot dobara khaali hota hai aur kisi aur ko mil sakta hai. Isse no-show kam hota hai. Is par poora tareeka agle article me hai: clinic me no-show kaise kam karein.

Aam galtiyan

  • Reminder subah 6 baje ya raat 10 baje bhejna. Samay sahi rakhiye.
  • Roz ek hi reminder bhejna, jisme koi info nahi hoti.
  • Reply nahi dekhna. Agar patient "2" bheje aur koi nahi dekhe, to ye reminder se bhi bura hai.
  • Sab patient ko ek hi message bhejna, chahe wo naya ho ya purana. Naye patient ko address aur kya lana hai ye batana zyada zaroori hai.

Receptionist ka roz ka routine (agar haath se karna ho)

  1. Shaam 5 baje kal ke sabhi appointment ki list kholiye.
  2. Har patient ko reminder bhejiye, aur jinhone confirm nahi kiya unke saamne ek chhota nishaan lagaiye.
  3. Subah 9 baje un patients ko call kijiye jinhone confirm nahi kiya.
  4. Cancel hue slot ko waitlist se bhariye.
  5. Din ke ant me dekhiye kitne aaye, kitne nahi aaye. Ye ek number likh lijiye.

Ye routine roz 25-30 minute leta hai. Jab ye routine bojh lagne lage, tab system ke baare me sochiye.

Naye aur purane patient ke liye alag message

Naye patient ko ye bataiye ki kya saath laana hai (purani report, prescription, ID), kitna pehle aana hai aur parking ya landmark kahan hai. Purane patient ke liye bas time aur confirm kaafi hai. Naye patient ka pehla bharosa isi message se banta hai, isliye wahan thoda zyada dhyan dijiye.

Doctor aur reception ko kya batayein

Naya system tabhi chalta hai jab doctor aur receptionist dono ko pata ho ki kya badla hai. Doctor ko batayein ki ab reminder patient ko apne aap jayega, aur confirm na karne wale patient ki list receptionist ke paas hogi. Receptionist ko batayein ki jawab dene wale "2" patients ko naya slot dena uska kaam hai. Aur dono ko ye bhi ki agar patient koi medical sawal likhe to uska jawab sirf doctor ya nurse dega, system nahi. Ye ek line ka niyam bahut si pareshaniyon se bachata hai.

Meri raay

Agar aapke clinic me roz 10 se zyada appointment hain aur kisi na kisi din slot khali rehta hai, to reminder pehla kaam hai jo lagana chahiye, kyunki iska asar jaldi dikhta hai. Par reminder aapke reception ke kaam ki jagah nahi leta. Wo ek tool hai jo receptionist ko doosre zaroori kaam ke liye free karta hai. Clinic ke liye poori detail clinics aur hospitals ke liye AI automation page par hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Clinic me WhatsApp reminder kitni baar bhejna chahiye?

Do baar kaafi hai: ek din pehle aur appointment se 2-3 ghante pehle. Visit ke baad ek dhanyavaad message optional hai.

Kya reminder me patient ki beemari likhni chahiye?

Nahi. Message me sirf naam, doctor, time aur address likhiye. Beemari ya report ki jaankari private hoti hai aur galat number par ja sakti hai.

Kya patient ki permission zaroori hai?

Haan, behtar hai ki registration par patient se WhatsApp reminder ki permission le lijiye. Data protection ke niyam ke liye apne vakeel ya CA se ek baar confirm kar lijiye.
