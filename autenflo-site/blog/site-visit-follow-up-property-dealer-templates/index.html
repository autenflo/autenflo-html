Site Visit Ke Baad Follow-up: Property Dealer Ke Message Templates

Site visit ke baad property customer ko kya message bhejein? 8 copy-paste templates: dhanyavaad, rate, parivaar ke liye aur hesitation par jawab.

Seedha jawab

Site visit ke baad pehle 2 ghante me ek chhota thank-you message bhejiye, agle din ek kaam ka sawal ya jaankari, aur 3 din baad ek saaf agla kadam. Bina dabaav ke, har message me ek hi baat. Neeche 8 templates hain jo aap seedha use kar sakte hain.

Is article me
  1. Site visit ke baad ke 4 niyam
  2. 8 templates
  3. Kab kya mat bhejiye
  4. Bachche hue sawal aur unke jawab
  5. Ye sab automatic kaise ho sakta hai
  6. Ye 3 cheezein follow-up ko behtar banati hain
  7. Site visit se pehle ki taiyari bhi follow-up ka hissa hai
  8. Meri raay

Site visit property ke business ka sabse bada kadam hai. Customer ne apne paise ka sapna zameen par dekha hai. Ab agle 48 ghante me wo ghar jaakar parivaar se baat karega, kisi aur dealer se bhi shayad milega, aur koi faisla lene ki taraf badhega. Jo dealer us waqt sahi message bhejta hai, wo deal ke kareeb rehta hai. Jo chup rehta hai, wo peeche chhoot jata hai. Neeche wahi sahi message hain, aur unke saath ye bhi ki kab kya nahi bhejna.

Site visit ke baad ke 4 niyam

  1. Pehle 2 ghante me ek message. Der na kijiye, par ghabrahat me turant 5 message bhi mat bhejiye.
  2. Har message me ek hi baat. Rate, ya sawal, ya agla kadam. Teeno ek saath nahi.
  3. Pressure nahi, madad. "Aaj hi book kar lijiye" ki jagah "kuch poochhna ho to batayein".
  4. Sach likhiye. Jo documents ya approval ki baat hai, wo wahi likhiye jo aap sach me jaante hain.

8 templates

1. Visit ke 2 ghante baad: dhanyavaad

Namaste [naam] ji, aaj [project / plot] dekhne aane ke liye dhanyavaad. Agar kuch poochhna ho, jaise rate, payment plan ya documents, to yahin likh dijiye, main aaram se jawab dunga.

2. Agle din: ek kaam ka sawal

[Naam] ji, kal aapko [plot / flat] kaisa laga? Location aur size ke baare me koi baat khatki ho to bata dijiye, main aapko behtar option bhi dikha sakta hu.

3. Rate aur payment plan bhejna

[Naam] ji, jaisa baat hui thi, [plot / flat] ka rate ₹[amount] hai. Payment plan: [booking amount], [installment ka ek chhota vivaran]. Registry aur baaki kharche [jo sach me lagenge wo likhiye]. Koi sawal ho to poochh lijiye.

4. Parivaar ko dikhane ka offer

[Naam] ji, agar ghar ke baaki log bhi site dekhna chahein to is [din] ko [time] par main khud saath chalunga. Aap bata dijiye, hum time tay kar lete hain.

5. Jab customer chup ho jaye (3-4 din baad)

[Naam] ji, bas ek chhota sa hal poochhna tha. Aap abhi [project] ke baare me soch rahe hain ya koi aur option dekh rahe hain? Jo bhi ho, seedha bata dijiye, main usi hisaab se madad karunga.

6. "Sochke batate hain" par

Bilkul [naam] ji, itna bada faisla soch samajhkar hi lena chahiye. Agar kisi cheez par doubt ho, jaise documents, location ya payment, to mujhe bata dijiye, main saaf jawab dunga. [Din] ko dobara baat karte hain.

7. "Rate zyada hai" par

[Naam] ji, aapki baat samajh aati hai. Is project me jo cheezein shamil hain, jaise [2-3 sach me wali baatein], unko dekhkar rate banaya gaya hai. Aap apna budget bata dijiye, main dekhta hu us me kya option ban sakta hai.

8. Aakhri message (agar customer jawab na de)

[Naam] ji, lagta hai abhi sahi samay nahi hai, koi baat nahi. Jab bhi [plot / flat] ke baare me baat karni ho, yahin message kar dijiye. Aapko shubhkamnayein.

Kab kya mat bhejiye

  • Roz message mat bhejiye. Customer pareshan ho jata hai aur number block kar deta hai.
  • "Aaj hi last day hai" jaisa jhootha pressure mat banaiye. Customer ko turant pata chal jata hai.
  • Doosre dealer ki burai mat kijiye. Isse aapka bharosa ghatta hai.
  • Customer ke parivaar ka mazaak mat udaiye. Property ka faisla ghar ka faisla hota hai.

Bachche hue sawal aur unke jawab

Site visit ke baad aksar customer ke 5 sawal hote hain: documents kya hain, registry kaun karwayega, loan milega ya nahi, pass me kya kya hai, aur booking cancel hui to paisa wapas kaise milega. Inke seedhe, saaf aur likhe hue jawab pehle se taiyar rakhiye. Jahan jawab aap par depend nahi karta, jaise loan ya legal salah, wahan kehiye ki ye bank ya vakeel se confirm kar lijiye. Ye aapki izzat badhata hai.

Ye sab automatic kaise ho sakta hai

Agar aap roz kai site visit karwate hain, to ye 8 messages haath se bhejna mushkil ho jata hai. Tab system visit hone ke baad apne aap thank-you, agle din ka sawal aur 3 din ka follow-up bhej sakta hai, aur aapko batata hai kaun lead garm hai. Rate, negotiation aur site par baat aap khud karte hain. Poora lead follow-up flow real estate lead follow-up guide me likha hai. Property ke liye humari alag page bhi hai.

Ye 3 cheezein follow-up ko behtar banati hain

  • Visit ke dauran notes. Customer ne kya poochha, usne kya pasand kiya. Follow-up me ye baat likhne se message "automatic" nahi, "personal" lagta hai.
  • Photo ya chhota video. Site ki ek photo ya project ka 15 second ka video message se zyada kaam karta hai.
  • Agla kadam saaf. Har message ke ant me ek hi sawal: "Kal 5 baje baat karein?"

Site visit se pehle ki taiyari bhi follow-up ka hissa hai

Achha follow-up visit se pehle hi shuru ho jata hai. Customer ko ek din pehle address, map link aur time bhejiye, aur poochhiye ki kitne log aayenge. Visit ke time par ek chhota notebook rakhiye aur likhiye ki customer ne kya poochha, kis cheez par ruka, kis baat par chup hua. Wahi notes baad me aapke follow-up ko personal bana dete hain. "Aapko jo road width ka sawal tha, uska jawab ye hai" jaisa message "dhanyavaad" wale message se kahin zyada kaam karta hai.

Meri raay

Property me log dealer se khareedte hain, plot se nahi. Isliye follow-up me aapka tone aur sach sabse bada kaam karta hai. Template ek dhaancha hai, uski jagah aapki apni baat zaroor daaliye. Aur ek baar me ek hi message, ek hi sawal: bas itna kaafi hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Site visit ke baad pehla message kab bhejna chahiye?

Visit ke 2 ghante ke andar ek chhota dhanyavaad message bhejiye, jisme aap kisi bhi sawal ke liye uplabdh hone ki baat kahein. Zyada jaldi ya zyada baar bhejna dabaav jaisa lagta hai.

Property customer jawab na de to kya karein?

3-4 din baad ek seedha sawal bhejiye, aur phir ek aakhri message. Uske baad customer ko dobara message mat bhejiye, jab tak wo khud na likhe.

Kya site visit follow-up automatic ho sakta hai?

Haan, thank-you, agle din ka sawal aur follow-up ka reminder automatic ho sakta hai. Rate, negotiation aur site par baat insaan ko hi karni chahiye.
