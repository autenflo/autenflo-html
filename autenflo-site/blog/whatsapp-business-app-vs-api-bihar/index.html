WhatsApp Business App Vs API: Bihar Ke Vyapari Kya Chunein

WhatsApp Business app free hai, API automation deta hai. Dono me farak, kharcha, seemayein aur Bihar ke vyapari ke liye kaun sa kab chunna chahiye.

Seedha jawab

WhatsApp Business app free hai aur roz 10-20 enquiry tak theek chalta hai. API tab chahiye jab enquiry zyada ho, kai log ek number sambhalein, ya aapko reminder aur update jaise template messages automatic bhejne hon. Pehle app, phir zaroorat padne par API.

Bihar ke vyapari ke liye ye sawal bada simple hai par jawab aksar ulta diya jata hai. Vendor bolta hai "API lijiye", free app wala bolta hai "app hi kaafi hai". Sach ye hai ki dono ki jagah hai. Ye article seedhe bata dega kaun sa kab chunna hai, aur kab nahi.

WhatsApp Business app: kya milta hai

Ye wahi app hai jo aap phone me download karte hain. Business ke liye iske kaam ke features:

  • Business profile: naam, address, timing, website, catalogue.
  • Quick replies: "/rate" likhte hi saved jawab.
  • Labels: customer ko "naya", "interested", "payment pending" me baantna.
  • Greeting aur away message: enquiry aate hi ya aap ke offline hone par.
  • Catalogue: products ya services ki list.
  • Ek main phone ke saath kuch linked devices par chalna.

Isme koi monthly kharcha nahi hota. Isliye jo log abhi shuru kar rahe hain, unke liye ye pehla kadam hai.

Business app ki seemayein

  • Sab kuch haath se. Quick reply bhi aapko khud chalani padti hai.
  • Follow-up automatic nahi. Kaun bhoola, ye aapko yaad rakhna padta hai.
  • Broadcast ki had. Broadcast list me aam taur par sirf 256 contacts aate hain, aur wo hi jinhone aapka number save kiya ho.
  • Reports nahi. Kitni enquiry aayi, kitni late hui, ye ginna haath se padta hai.
  • Team ke saath mushkil. Kai log ek number par chat dekhte hain to kaun kya bola, ye confusion ban jata hai.

WhatsApp Business API: kya badalta hai

API me aap seedha Meta ke platform se jude hote hain, aam taur par kisi software ya partner ke through. Isse ye sab mumkin hota hai:

  • Automatic jawab: sawal ke hisaab se jawab, bina insaan ke.
  • Template messages: appointment reminder, payment reminder, order update, jo aap pehle se Meta se approve karwate hain.
  • Ek number, kai log: team ek dashboard se chat sambhalti hai.
  • Follow-up aur tagging: system khud yaad dilata hai.
  • Data: enquiry kahan se aayi, kitni der me reply gaya, ye sab record me.

Bihar ke vyapari ke liye kaun sa kab

App chuniye agar

  • Roz 10-20 se kam enquiry aati hai.
  • Aap khud ya ek bande se reply sambhal jaata hai.
  • Abhi sirf rate aur timing ke sawal aate hain.

API chuniye agar

  • Enquiry roz 30-40 se upar jaa rahi hai aur reply late ho raha hai.
  • Appointment, payment ya order reminder bhejna hai, jaise clinic aur coaching.
  • Teen se zyada log ek hi number se baat karte hain.
  • Aap pata karna chahte hain ki kaun si enquiry chhoot rahi hai.

API ke chhupe kharche aur dhyan dene wali baatein

API lene se pehle ye cheezein dekh lijiye:

  1. Template message ka Meta charge. Ye category ke hisaab se lagta hai. Rate Meta ki pricing page par dekhiye.
  2. 24 ghante ki window. Customer ke message ke baad 24 ghante tak aap kuch bhi likh sakte hain. Uske baad sirf approved template bhej sakte hain.
  3. Opt-in. Customer ki permission ke bina marketing message nahi bhejna chahiye.
  4. Number ki migration. Agar aap apne purane number par API lagana chahte hain to pehle chat history ka backup lijiye aur partner se pooch lijiye ki us number ka kya hoga.

Business app ko 10 minute me sahi se set kaise karein

  1. WhatsApp Business app me business profile bhariye: naam, category, address, timing aur ek line ka description.
  2. Settings me Business tools kholkar Greeting message aur Away message chaalu kijiye.
  3. Quick replies banaiye: rate, address, timing, payment details, shukriya. Har ek ka ek chhota shortcut rakhiye.
  4. Labels banaiye: naya, interested, order, payment pending.
  5. Catalogue me apne 10-20 sabse zyada bikne wale product ya service jodiye.

Ye 10 minute ka kaam hai aur aapke sau me se 90 jawab ek hi jagah aa jaate hain.

API par jaane se pehle apne se 5 sawal

  1. Kya mere sawal-jawab likhe hue hain, ya abhi bhi dimaag me hain?
  2. Kya mujhe pata hai ki roz kitni enquiry aati hai?
  3. Kya mujhe reminder ya update messages bhejne hain?
  4. Kya team me kai log ek hi number sambhalte hain?
  5. Kya main aane wale 3 mahine tak is par dhyan de sakta hu?

Agar chaar jawab "haan" hain, to API ka samay aa gaya hai. Agar do se kam, to pehle app sudhaarein.

Shuru karne wale chhote dukandaar ki ek aam galti

Bahut se vyapari sochte hain ki API lene se unka kaam apne aap ho jayega. Aisa nahi hota. API ek mashin ki tarah hai: jab tak aap use sahi jawab nahi sikhate, wo bhi sirf wahi dohrayegi jo aapne likha hai. Jo business apni rate list, timing aur payment ka tareeka pehle likh leta hai, uske liye API fayda deti hai. Jo nahi likhta, uske liye API sirf ek mehnga dashboard ban jati hai. Isliye pehle aapko likhna seekhna hai, system baad me aata hai.

Meri raay

Bihar ke 80% chhote business ko API ki zaroorat shuru me nahi hoti. Pehle 2-3 mahine app par jawab aur follow-up ka anushaasan banaiye. Agar tab bhi aap reply me peeche rehte hain, ya reminder bhejna zaroori ho gaya hai, to API par jaayiye. Ek aur baat: vendor agar bina aapka process dekhe "API lagana padega" bol de, to ek aur raay lijiye.

Agar aap apni jagah ke hisaab se samajhna chahte hain to Bihar ke business ke liye WhatsApp automation guide padhiye, aur plans me dekhiye ki hum kya kya sambhalte hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

WhatsApp Business app aur API me sabse bada farak kya hai?

App me sab kuch aap haath se karte hain, API me reply, follow-up aur reminder system khud karta hai. API me template messages aur team dashboard bhi milte hain, par uska kharcha lagta hai.

Kya main apne purane WhatsApp number par API laga sakta hu?

Haan, par us number ko pehle personal app se hatana padta hai. Chat history ka backup lekar aur apne partner se migration ka tareeka samajhkar hi aage badhiye.

Kya API ke baad customer ko alag number par message aayega?

Nahi, customer ko wahi business number dikhta hai. Farak sirf aapki taraf ka hota hai: aap kaise reply sambhalte hain.
