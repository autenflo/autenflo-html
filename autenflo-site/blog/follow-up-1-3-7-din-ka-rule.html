Follow-up ka 1-3-7 din ka rule: ek enquiry order kaise banti hai

Enquiry ke baad 1, 3 aur 7 din par kya bhejein, bina customer ko pareshan kiye. Teen chhote message templates ke saath.

Bahut si enquiry isliye order nahi banti kyunki ek hi reply ke baad baat khatam ho jati hai. Customer busy tha, bhool gaya, ya doosre se bhi puch raha tha.

Din 1: kaam ki yaad

“Namaste [naam] ji, kal aapne [cheez] ke baare me poochha tha. Kuch aur jaankari chahiye to bataiye.”

Din 3: ek kaam ki cheez

“[Naam] ji, [rate / batch / slot] ki ek chhoti jaankari bhej raha hu. Aap chahein to kal [time] par baat kar lein?”

Din 7: saaf aur izzat se aakhri message

“[Naam] ji, agar abhi sahi samay nahi hai to koi baat nahi. Jab bhi zaroorat ho, yahin message kar dijiye.”

  • Har message chhota rakhiye.
  • Jo mana kare, use dobara message mat bhejiye.
  • Sirf unhe message bhejiye jinhone khud aapko likha ho.

Ye kaam roz haath se karna mushkil hota hai. Isiliye isse automate karna sabse pehla faayda deta hai.
