WhatsApp par customer ko 60 second me reply kaise dein

Enquiry aate hi reply dene se order kyun badhte hain, aur chhote business ye kaam bina naya staff rakhe kaise kar sakte hain.

Jo customer pehle reply paata hai, wahi aksar order bhi de deta hai. Phir bhi zyada tar business me enquiry ka reply tab jata hai jab staff ko yaad aata hai.

Reply late hone ki 3 wajah

  1. Enquiry WhatsApp, call aur Instagram par bikhri hoti hai.
  2. Wahi sawal roz poochhe jaate hain: price, timing, address.
  3. Raat ko aur chhutti ke din koi reply dene wala nahi hota.

Ye kaam aap kaise sambhal sakte hain

  • Sabse zyada poochhe jaane wale 10 sawal aur unke jawab ek jagah likh lijiye.
  • Har enquiry ko ek sheet me darj kijiye, taaki koi chhoote nahi.
  • Hot leads ko alag mark kijiye aur unka follow-up 1, 3 aur 7 din par rakhiye.
  • Jo kaam roz dohrata hai, use AI se karwaiye aur sirf zaroori baat apne paas rakhiye.
Har bhoola hua follow-up, ek bika hua order hai.
