Coaching Me Admission Enquiry Ka Reply Kaise Karein (Templates Ke Saath)

Coaching admission enquiry ka reply kaise karein: fees, batch timing, demo aur "sochke batate hain" ke 8 copy-paste templates aur aam galtiyan.

Seedha jawab

Admission enquiry ka reply 5 minute ke andar jaye, pehli line me sawal ka seedha jawab ho, aur ant me ek hi agla kadam ho, jaise demo class. Parent ko fees sabse pehle chahiye, to use chhupaiye mat. Niche 8 templates hain jo aap seedha copy karke use kar sakte hain.

Admission enquiry ka reply ek chhota kaam lagta hai, par coaching ka sabse bada paisa yahin se aata hai. Parent ne aapko aur do aur coaching ko ek saath message kiya hai. Jo sabse pehle, saaf aur izzat se jawab deta hai, parent ke dimaag me wahi pehle aata hai. Neeche 8 situations ke templates hain, aur har ek ke saath ek chhoti galti jo log karte hain.

Pehle 4 niyam jo har reply me lagu hote hain

  1. Pehle 5 minute. Zyada tar parent jis coaching se pehle jawab milta hai, usse baat aage badhate hain.
  2. Pehli line me jawab. "Namaste" ke baad seedha wo cheez jo poochhi gayi hai.
  3. Fees chhupaiye mat. Parent ko pata hota hai ki fees poochhni hai. Jo coaching fees se bachti hai, parent ko laga hai ki kuch chhupa rahe hain.
  4. Ek hi agla kadam. "Aap kal 5 baje demo ke liye aa sakte hain?" Ye "kabhi aaiye" se kahin behtar hai.

8 templates

1. Pehla reply (jab bas "details chahiye" aaya ho)

Namaste [parent ka naam] ji, [coaching ka naam] me aapka swagat hai. Kis class aur subject ke liye jaankari chahiye? Bata dijiye to batch timing, fees aur demo class ki details seedha bhej dete hain.

2. Fees poochhne par

[Class] ke liye [batch] ki fees ₹[amount] hai, jisme [kya kya shamil hai]. Aap [installment / ek saath] me bhar sakte hain. Seat sankhya seemit hai, isliye pehle demo class dekh lein, phir faisla kijiye. Kal [time] par demo hai, aa sakte hain?

3. Batch timing poochhne par

[Class] ke batches: subah [time] aur shaam [time]. Aapke bachche ki school timing ke hisaab se jo sahi ho wo bata dijiye, hum seat check karke bata dete hain.

4. Demo class ka invite

[Bachche ka naam] ke liye demo class [din, date] ko [time] par hai, [address / map link]. Kuch laane ki zaroorat nahi. Bachche ke saath aap bhi aa sakte hain. Kal subah ek yaad dilane wala message bhej denge.

5. Demo ke baad "sochke batate hain" par

Bilkul, aaram se soch lijiye. Agar koi sawal ho, jaise teacher, test series ya doubt class, to yahin likh dijiye. [Din] tak batch me [kuch] seat bachi hain, isliye bata deta hu.

6. "Doosri coaching sasti hai" par

Ji, har coaching ki fees alag hoti hai. Hamare yahan [do-teen asli cheezein, jaise batch ka size, test frequency, doubt class] hai. Aap dono jagah ka demo dekh lijiye, phir jo bachche ko samajh aaye wahi chuniye.

7. Scholarship ya fees me chhoot poochhne par

[Scholarship test / chhoot ke niyam] ke baare me poori jaankari [din] ko [office] me di jati hai. Aap [date] ko test ke liye aa sakte hain, ya hum details yahin bhej dete hain.

8. Raat ke time (jab aap offline hon)

Namaste, aapka message mil gaya. Abhi hamari office band hai, subah [time] se pehle aapko poori jaankari mil jayegi. Tab tak [fees / batch / demo] ke baare me bata dijiye ki aap kya jaanna chahte hain.

Jo galtiyan parents ko door karti hain

  • Lamba paragraph. Mobile par koi nahi padhta.
  • "Aap office aa jaiye." Bina baat kiye. Pehle jawab do, phir bulao.
  • Dara kar bechna. "Seat khatam ho rahi hai" tabhi likhiye jab sach ho.
  • Doosri coaching ki burai. Isse parent ka bharosa ghatta hai.
  • Bina naam ke message. "Dear Sir" ya "Dear Parent" yaad nahi rehta.

Parents ke sawal kahan se dhoondhein

Apne sawal-jawab ki list banane ke liye teen jagah dekhiye: pichhle mahine ke WhatsApp chats (sabse zyada poochhe jaane wale 10 sawal), reception ya counter par aate sawal, aur Google Business Profile par aaye comments aur sawal. Teen jagah se milkar aapke 15 asli sawal banenge. Bina kisi tool ke ye 30 minute ka kaam hai.

Ye sab kaise automatic ho sakta hai

Agar roz 15-20 se zyada enquiry aati hai, to ye templates haath se bhejna thaka dega, aur raat ke message chhoot jaayenge. Tab system pehla reply, demo booking aur follow-up sambhal sakta hai. Poora admission se fees tak ka flow yahan likha hai. Aur coaching institutes ke liye humara page dekhiye.

Mummy, papa aur student: teeno se alag baat

Coaching ki enquiry teen jagah se aati hai, aur teeno ka tone alag hota hai. Papa aksar fees, result aur safety poochhte hain, to unhe seedha aur number wala jawab dijiye. Mummy aksar bachche ki dekhbhaal, timing aur teacher ke baare me poochhti hain, to unhe bharosa dene wali baat kijiye. Student khud message kare to wo chhota, casual aur seedha jawab chahta hai. Ek hi template teeno par fit nahi hota, isliye pehle message me hi poochh lijiye ki baat kis se ho rahi hai: "Aap [bachche ke] parent hain ya student?"

Raat ke message kaun dekhe

Raat ke message ka reply subah 9 baje tak jana hi chahiye. Ek insaan ko ye zimmedari dijiye: "Subah ka pehla kaam raat ke enquiries ka jawab." Agar ye tay nahi hota, to ye message kisi ke bhi zimme nahi rehta, aur parent ek hi raat me doosri coaching se baat kar chuka hota hai.

Ek aakhri baat

Template wahi kaam karta hai jo sach ho. Fees, seat aur teacher ke baare me jo likhiye, wo wahi ho jo aap nibha sakte hain. Parent ek baar bharosa tod de to usse wapas jodna bahut mushkil hota hai. Chhota, saaf aur sach: bas itna.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Admission enquiry ka reply kitni der me dena chahiye?

Jitna jaldi ho utna behtar, aam taur par 5 minute ke andar. Raat ko offline hone par ek automatic message jisme subah tak jawab ka wada ho, kaafi hai.

Kya fees pehle message me batani chahiye?

Haan. Parent fees ke baare me poochhne ke liye hi message karta hai. Fees batakar demo ka offer dena zyada bharosa banata hai bajaye fees chhupane ke.

Parent "sochke batate hain" bole to kya karein?

Ek din baad ek naram follow-up bhejiye jisme demo ka feedback ya batch me bachi seat ki jaankari ho. Bar bar message mat bhejiye, 1, 3 aur 7 din ka rule kaafi hai.
