WhatsApp Par Customer Ko Reply Kaise Dein: 7 Zaroori Rules

WhatsApp par customer ko reply kaise dein: 7 zaroori rules jo order badhate hain, kitni der me reply dein, kya likhein aur kin galtiyon se bachein.

Seedha jawab

Customer ko WhatsApp par jaldi reply dijiye (5 minute ke andar), pehli line me seedha jawab, naam se baat shuru kijiye, ek hi agla sawal poochhiye, aur raat ke liye ek saaf message rakhiye. Ye 7 rules chhote business ke order aur bharosa dono badhate hain.

WhatsApp par customer ko reply dena ek aam kaam lagta hai, isliye usme sabse zyada galtiyan hoti hain. Koi raat ko jawab nahi deta, koi ek-ek line me jawab deta hai, koi kal ka reply parso deta hai. Aur customer chupchap doosri dukaan chala jata hai, bina kuch kahe. Ye 7 rules chhote business ke liye banaye hain, aur inme koi bhaari cheez nahi hai.

Rule 1: Pehle 5 minute me reply dijiye

Customer jab message karta hai to wo us waqt aapke baare me soch raha hota hai. 5 minute baad wo kisi aur dukaan ko message kar chuka hota hai. Aap har waqt phone par nahi baith sakte, isliye ek turant "aapka message mil gaya" wala reply rakhiye, jo aapke khaali hone par jaye. Isse customer ko pata chalta hai ki aap hain, aur wo thoda rukta hai.

Rule 2: Pehli line me seedha jawab

Customer ne rate poochha to pehli line me rate. "Namaste, kaise hain aap, bahut bahut dhanyavaad hamari dukaan se judne ke liye" jaisa lamba intro mat likhiye. Customer ne jo poochha hai wahi pehle batao, baad me baaki baat.

Rule 3: Naam se shuru kijiye

"Namaste Rakesh ji" aur "Namaste Sir" me bada farak hai. Naam se customer ko lagta hai ki aap use pehchante hain. Agar naam nahi pata to pehle message me poochh lijiye. WhatsApp Business app me contact ka naam save karna aur use message me daalna aasan hai.

Rule 4: Chhota likhiye

Mobile par 4-5 line se zyada koi nahi padhta. Agar jawab lamba hai to usse 2 hisson me baantiye, ya list banaiye. Ek lamba paragraph bhejne se customer "baad me padhunga" kehkar bhool jata hai.

Rule 5: Ek hi agla sawal poochhiye

"Kya chahiye, kitna chahiye, kab chahiye, kahan bhejna hai, kaise payment karenge?" Paanch sawal ek saath poochhne se customer jawab hi nahi deta. Ek sawal poochhiye: "Kitna quantity chahiye?" Jawab milte hi agla.

Rule 6: Raat aur chhutti ke liye ek message rakhiye

Aap hamesha online nahi reh sakte. Ek away message set kijiye: "Abhi hamari dukaan band hai, subah [time] se pehle aapko jawab milega. Tab tak aap [rate / address / timing] bata dijiye." Ye message customer ko rok leta hai. Aur subah ke pehle kaam me raat ke messages ka reply zaroor dijiye.

Rule 7: Jo vaada kiya wo nibhaiye

Agar aapne kaha ki "ek ghante me bata dete hain", to ek ghante me bataiye. Customer vaada yaad rakhta hai. Jo bhi baat aap sach me nahi nibha sakte (delivery ka samay, stock, rate), wo likhiye mat. Ek jhoota wada customer ka bharosa tod deta hai, aur WhatsApp par wo baat aapke naam se doosre ko bhi jaati hai.

In rules ko kaise lagayein: ek hafte ka plan

  1. Din 1: Away message aur greeting message set kijiye.
  2. Din 2: 10 aam sawalon ke jawab quick replies me save kijiye.
  3. Din 3: Staff ko batayein ki pehla reply 5 minute me jana chahiye, aur kaun dekhega.
  4. Din 4-5: Har enquiry par label lagaiye.
  5. Din 6: Apne dost se alag alag samay par sawal bhijwakar test kijiye.
  6. Din 7: Gin lijiye kitne reply 5 minute me gaye, kitne late.

Kab ye haath se mushkil ho jata hai

Jab roz 20-30 se zyada enquiry aane lagti hai, to ye rules haath se nibhana mushkil ho jata hai. Staff thak jata hai, ya raat ke message chhoot jaate hain. Tab system pehla reply, jawab aur follow-up sambhal sakta hai, aur aap sirf mushkil sawal dekhte hain. Is par seedha tareeka kahan se shuru karein me hai. Aur plans me dekhiye ki hum kya sambhalte hain.

Ye rules teen alag business par kaise dikhte hain

Kapde ki dukaan

Customer photo bhejta hai aur poochhta hai "ye wala hai?". Reply me pehle haan ya na, phir size aur rate, aur ant me ek sawal: "Kaun si size chahiye?" Photo ka jawab photo se dijiye.

Clinic

Patient poochhta hai "kal doctor sahab hain?". Pehli line me haan ya na aur samay, phir ek hi sawal: "Kal [time] ka slot book kar dun?". Beemari ki baat message me nahi.

Coaching

Parent poochhta hai "fees kitni hai?". Pehli line me fees, phir demo ka offer. Naam se baat shuru, aur raat ke message ka reply subah ke pehle kaam me.

WhatsApp Business app ke 3 features jo in rules me madad karte hain

  • Quick replies: 10 aam jawab ek shortcut se.
  • Away message: raat aur chhutti ke liye.
  • Labels: kaun naya, kaun interested, kaun payment pending, ye ek nazar me.
