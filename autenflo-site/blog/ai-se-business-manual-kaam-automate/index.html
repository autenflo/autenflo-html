AI Se Business Ke 5 Manual Kaam Automate Kaise Karein

AI se business ke 5 manual kaam automate kaise karein: enquiry entry, follow-up, invoice, reminder aur roz ka report, pehle-baad ke hisaab ke saath.

Seedha jawab

Business ke 5 manual kaam jo AI se automate ho sakte hain: enquiry ki entry, follow-up, invoice banana, payment reminder aur roz ka report. Pehle apna process kaagaz par likhiye, phir har kaam ka tareeka tay kijiye, aur jahan insaan ka dimaag chahiye wahan manual hi rakhiye.

Zyada tar business owners ka din aise guzarta hai: subah register kholna, enquiry likhna, customer ko phone karna, shaam ko bill banana, aur raat ko hisaab milana. Ye sab kaam zaroori hain, par inme se bahut se roz same tareeke se hote hain. Aur jo kaam roz same tareeke se hota hai, wahi AI se automate karne layak hai. Is article me 5 aise kaam hain, aur har ek ke saath pehle-baad ka seedha hisaab.

Pehle ye jaanch lijiye: kaun sa kaam automate karne layak hai

Kisi bhi kaam par ye 4 sawal poochhiye:

  1. Kya ye kaam roz ya har hafte hota hai?
  2. Kya iske steps hamesha lagbhag same hote hain?
  3. Kya isme galti hone par nuksaan chhota hai?
  4. Kya isme insaan ki samajh ya mol-bhav ki zaroorat nahi hai?

Jis kaam par 4 me se 3 jawab "haan" ho, wo automate karne layak hai.

Kaam 1: Enquiry ki entry

Pehle: Customer ka message aata hai, staff register ya sheet me naam, number aur kya poochha likhta hai. Kabhi kabhi likhna bhool jata hai, ya galat number likh deta hai.

Baad me: Enquiry aate hi naam, number, kya poochha aur kab, ye sab ek sheet me apne aap bhar jata hai. Kaise shuru karein: Pehle ek sheet banaiye jisme 6 columns hon (naam, number, kya poochha, kab, kahan se, stage). Phir WhatsApp ya form se aati jaankari ko us me bharne ka tareeka dhoondhiye.

Kaam 2: Follow-up

Pehle: Owner ko yaad rakhna padta hai ki kisne poochha tha aur kis ko dobara message karna hai. Bahut se customer isliye chhoot jaate hain.

Baad me: Har enquiry ko 1, 3 aur 7 din par apne aap message jata hai. Jo jawab de de wo aapke paas aa jata hai. Templates ke liye follow-up message templates dekhiye.

Kaam 3: Invoice aur bill

Pehle: Order ke baad staff bill banata hai, rate aur total haath se nikalta hai, aur customer ko WhatsApp par photo bhejta hai.

Baad me: Order confirm hote hi item, quantity aur rate se invoice ka PDF apne aap ban jata hai aur customer ko chala jata hai. Isme galti ka khatra kam hota hai kyunki rate ek hi jagah se aata hai. Kaise shuru karein: koi billing app ya Google Sheets ka invoice template chuniye aur usi ko chalana seekhiye.

Kaam 4: Payment reminder

Pehle: Owner ya staff phone karke paise maangta hai. Ye awkward lagta hai, aur kabhi kabhi rishta bigad jata hai.

Baad me: Due date se pehle aur uske do-teen din baad ek naram message customer ko jata hai. Jo payment kar de, uska reminder band ho jata hai. Kaise shuru karein: ek list banaiye ki kisne kitna dena hai, kab tak, aur do templates likhiye.

Namaste [naam] ji, ₹[amount] ka payment [date] ko due hai. UPI: [id]. Ho chuka hai to screenshot bhej dijiye, nahi hua to batayein, hum madad kar sakte hain.

Kaam 5: Roz ka report

Pehle: Owner din ke ant me sab kuch pooch kar ya register dekhkar hisaab banata hai: aaj kitni enquiry aayi, kitni bikri hui, kitna payment baaki hai.

Baad me: Roz shaam ek chhota summary aapke WhatsApp par aa jata hai. Kaise shuru karein: pehle ye 4-5 numbers tay kijiye jo aap roz dekhna chahte hain. Phir inhe sheet se nikalne ka tareeka banaiye.

Ye 5 kaam kitna time bachate hain

Ye aapke business par depend karta hai, isliye koi bhi vendor jo sabko ek hi number bataye, usse sawal kijiye. Aap khud apna hisaab nikaal sakte hain: har kaam ko roz kitni der lagti hai, us ko 26 din se guna karke dekhiye. Maan lijiye enquiry entry me 30 minute, follow-up me 40, bill me 30, reminder me 20 aur report me 20 minute. Ye 140 minute yani 2 ghante 20 minute roz hote hain, ya mahine me lagbhag 60 ghante. Ye ek udaharan hai, aapke number alag honge. Par isse ye samajh aata hai ki chhote chhote kaam milkar ek insaan ka bada hissa kha jaate hain.

Jahan automation nahi karna chahiye

  • Jab process hi likha nahi hai. Pehle likhiye.
  • Jab customer ka sawal har baar alag hota hai. Wahan insaan ki baat zaroori hai.
  • Jab ek galti ka nuksaan bahut bada ho. Jaise bade rate ka quotation, bina check kiye mat bhejiye.
  • Jab aapko khud pata nahi ki kaam kaise hota hai. Pehle us kaam ko 2 hafte khud dekhiye.

Shuru kaise karein: 7 din ka plan

  1. Din 1: Ek kaagaz par apne roz ke 10 kaam likhiye.
  2. Din 2: Har kaam par upar ke 4 sawal lagaiye.
  3. Din 3: Top 2 kaam chuniye.
  4. Din 4-5: Un 2 kaamon ke steps likhiye, jaise kisi naye bande ko sikhane ke liye.
  5. Din 6: Un steps ko sheet, WhatsApp Business app ya kisi tool me daaliye.
  6. Din 7: Ek hafte chalakar dekhiye aur sudhaar kijiye.

Kab khud karein, kab kisi se karwayein

Agar aapke paas time hai aur kaam seedha hai, to ek ek karke khud shuru kijiye. Agar aapko 3-4 kaam ek saath chahiye, ya WhatsApp API aur multiple tools ko jodna hai, to kisi aise partner se karwaiye jo aapka process dekhe, phir quote de. autenflo ka setup ₹14,999 se aur monthly ₹5,999 se shuru hota hai, aur pehla process aam taur par 2-4 hafte me live hota hai. Meri raay

Automation ka matlab hai ki aap apne business me insaan ki jagah sahi kaam par laga sakein. Jo kaam roz same tareeke se hota hai, use system ko dijiye. Jo kaam me sochna, baat karna aur faisla lena hai, use aap khud karte rahiye. Aur ek baar me ek hi kaam shuru kijiye, kyunki jo ek kaam theek se chal gaya, wahi aage ka bharosa banata hai.
