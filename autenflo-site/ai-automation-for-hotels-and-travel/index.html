Hotels, Travel aur Tours me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Hotel aur travel ka business season par chalta hai, aur season me sabse zyada nuksan late jawab se hota hai. Rate, availability, advance aur pickup ka kaam alag-alag log alag-alag jagah sambhalte hain. Yahan wo 6 jagah hain jahan hotel, homestay aur cab operator ki booking chhootti hai.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Rate aur availability ke har sawal ka manual jawab

Problem: Roz 40 log poochte hain "kal ke liye room hai kya, kitna lagega". Har ek ko alag se jawab dena reception ka aadha din kha jata hai.

autenflo: Availability aur rate turant jata hai, aur booking ke liye agla step bhi saath hi.

02

Booking details register me typing

Problem: Naam, ID, check-in aur advance sab register me haath se likhe jate hain. Galti hui to check-in par jhagda hota hai.

autenflo: Booking ki details chat se apne aap record hoti hain, ek jagah.

03

Confirmation, documents aur payment ka follow-up

Problem: Advance aaya ya nahi, ID mili ya nahi, ye sab yaad rakhna padta hai. Kai booking sirf follow-up ke bina cancel ho jati hain.

autenflo: Confirmation message, document ki list aur advance reminder auto jata hai, aur payment aate hi booking pakki.

04

Check-in, pickup aur staff ka coordination

Problem: Guest ko pickup chahiye, room taiyar hona chahiye, driver ko time batana hai. Sab phone par chalta hai aur kuch na kuch chhoot jata hai.

autenflo: Check-in se pehle guest aur staff dono ko reminder, aur pickup ka time aur driver ka number auto.

05

Season ke baad guest follow-up aur feedback

Problem: Guest chala gaya aur baat khatam. Na feedback, na dobara aane ki baat.

autenflo: Stay ke baad feedback ka message aur agli trip ka offer apne aap jata hai.

06

Daily occupancy aur revenue report manually

Problem: Aaj kitne room bhare, kitna aaya, kitna baaki hai, ye jodne me roz ghanta lagta hai.

autenflo: Roz shaam ko occupancy aur revenue ka summary owner ko WhatsApp par aa jata hai.

Mann me jo sawal hain

Hotels, Travel aur Tours: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Hotel ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye rate aur availability turant batata hai, booking record karta hai, advance ka reminder bhejta hai aur stay ke baad feedback leta hai. Sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par hota hai.

Kya chhote guest house aur homestay ke liye bhi chalega?

Haan. Chhote guest house me reception par aksar ek hi insaan hota hai, isliye season ki bheed me WhatsApp ka jawab sabse pehle chhootta hai. Automation yahi sambhalta hai.

Cab aur tour operators ke liye ye kaise kaam aata hai?

Route aur rate ka jawab, booking record, driver ka detail aur pickup reminder auto jata hai. Tours ki booking bhi isi tarah sambhal sakte hain.

Season me ek saath 100 enquiry aaye to?

AI ek saath kitni bhi chats sambhal leta hai. Har yatri ko rate aur availability milti hai, aur jo booking karna chahe uska alert aapko aata hai.

Kya advance aur payment ke reminder jayenge?

Haan. Advance ka reminder aur confirmation auto jata hai, aur payment aate hi booking pakki ho jati hai. Aap tay karte ho kitni baar reminder jaye.

Kya Booking.com ya MakeMyTrip ke bookings ke saath chalega?

Jo guest WhatsApp par aata hai uska pura kaam sambhalta hai. Portal bookings ko jodne ka tareeka hum setup ke waqt dekhte hain.

Kya guest ko Hindi me jawab milega?

Haan, Hindi me aur zaroorat par English me. Bahar se aaye yatri ko rasta, parking aur check-in ka time ek message me mil jata hai.

Kya booking details surakshit rahengi?

Guest ki details aapke apne account me rehti hain aur sirf booking ke kaam ke liye use hoti hain. Hum privacy policy me likhe niyam follow karte hain.

Feedback aur repeat booking ke liye kya hota hai?

Stay ke baad guest ko feedback ka message jata hai, aur agle season me offer. Isse purane guest wapas aate hain.
