Professional Services & Agencies me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Professional services ka business aapke time aur client ke bharose par chalta hai. Lekin aadha time documents maangne, due date yaad dilane, status batane aur fees ke reminder me jata hai. Ye sab rules par chalne wale kaam hain, isliye automate ho sakte hain. Neeche wahi 6 jagah hain jahan aapka time aur paisa chhootta hai.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Naye client ki enquiry aur consultation ke liye baar-baar calls

Problem: Naya client poochhta hai "GST registration ka kya process hai, fees kitni?". Aap meeting me the, jawab late gaya, aur client ne dusre se baat kar li.

autenflo: Aapki services, documents aur fees ki jaankari turant jati hai, consultation ka slot auto book hota hai aur serious client ka alert seedha aapko aata hai.

02

Documents ek-ek karke maangna aur yaad dilana

Problem: PAN, Aadhaar, bank statement: har client se alag-alag maangna padta hai. Kaun sa document aaya aur kaun sa baaki hai, ye yaad rakhna mushkil hota hai.

autenflo: Client ko checklist jati hai, document aate hi record me jud jata hai, aur jo baaki hai uska reminder tameez se auto jata hai.

03

GST, ITR aur renewal ki due date yaad dilana

Problem: Due date nikal gayi to penalty client ki, par naraazgi aapse. Sau clients ki dates diary me likhna aur yaad dilana roz ka kaam hai.

autenflo: Due date se pehle client ko reminder auto jata hai, aur aap ko roz ka list milta hai ki kis client ka kaam kahan atka hai.

04

Kaam ho gaya, fees atki hai

Problem: Kaam pura hua par fees maangne me jhijhak hoti hai. Invoice banana, bhejna aur baar-baar poochna, teeno me time jata hai.

autenflo: Kaam hote hi invoice auto banta hai aur client ko jata hai, due date par tameez ka reminder jata hai, aur roz ka outstanding summary aapko milta hai.

05

Client poochta hai: mera kaam kahan tak pahuncha?

Problem: Har client ko status batane ke liye call ya message karna padta hai. Staff ko kaam dene ke baad bhi baar-baar poochna padta hai ki kahan tak hua.

autenflo: Kaam ka status client ko WhatsApp par auto milta hai, tasks staff ko assign hote hain, aur agencies ke liye client ko monthly report auto jati hai.

06

Purane clients ko wapas lana, referral maangna

Problem: Purana client saal bhar baad kisi aur CA ya agency ke paas chala jata hai, kyunki beech me koi sampark nahi tha. Apni pehchaan ka content banane ka time nahi milta.

autenflo: Renewal aur saalana kaam ka reminder auto jata hai, khush client se feedback aur referral maanga jata hai, aur content calendar aur auto-posting se aapki pehchaan banti hai.

Professional Services & Agencies: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Professional services ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye naye client ki enquiry ka turant jawab deta hai, documents aur due date ka reminder bhejta hai, invoice aur fees ka reminder chalata hai, aur kaam ka status client ko deta hai. Sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par chalta hai.

Kya AI tax ya legal salah dega?

Nahi. AI sirf jaankari, checklist aur reminder ka kaam karta hai. Tax, legal ya case ki salah wale sawal seedha aap ya aapke staff tak jate hain.

Kya chhote CA, vakeel ya akele consultant ke liye bhi chalega?

Haan. Akele professional par follow-up aur reminder ka poora bojh hota hai, isliye automation ka sabse zyada fayda wahi dekhte hain.

Agencies (marketing, IT, design) ke liye kya hai?

Client ki enquiry, proposal ka follow-up, kaam ka status, monthly report aur invoice ka reminder automate hota hai. Client ko baar-baar update nahi dena padta.

Client ke documents aur confidential jaankari ka kya?

Client ka data aapke apne account me rehta hai aur sirf aapke kaam ke liye use hota hai. Hum privacy policy me likhe niyam follow karte hain, aur sensitive baat AI khud nahi sambhalta.

Due date ke reminder kaise kaam karte hain?

Aap client ki dates ek baar daalte ho. AI due date se pehle client ko tameez se reminder bhejta hai, aur aapko roz ki pending list deta hai.

Fees collection me kya madad milegi?

Kaam hone par invoice auto jata hai, due date par reminder jata hai, aur payment aate hi status update ho jata hai. Aap tay karte ho kitni baar aur kis tone me reminder jaye.

Kya ye mere accounting ya practice software ke saath chalega?

Aksar haan, kyunki ye WhatsApp aur aapki sheet ya software ke beech kaam karta hai. Pehle hum aapka process dekhte hain, phir batate hain kaise judega.

Setup me kitna time lagta hai?

Pehla process aam taur par 2 se 4 hafte me live ho jata hai. Aap bas batate ho ki kaam kaise chalta hai.
