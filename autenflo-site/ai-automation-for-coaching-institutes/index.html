Coaching, Tuition aur Schools me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Coaching ka business teen cheezon par chalta hai: enquiry kitni aayi, demo me kitne aaye, aur fees time par aayi ya nahi. Teeno jagah kaam manual hota hai: register, call aur WhatsApp. Is page me hum wahi jagah dikhate hain jahan coaching aur school ka paisa aur time leak hota hai, aur har jagah AI kya karta hai.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Enquiry register me, jawab kal

Problem: Parents ne raat 10 baje fees poochi. Reception subah 9 baje khulta hai. Tab tak parents 2 aur coaching se baat kar chuke hote hain. Enquiry diary me likhne wala bhi roz ek insaan chahiye.

autenflo: AI turant batch, fees, timing aur demo slot batata hai. Har enquiry ek jagah naam, class, area aur source ke saath apne aap record hoti hai.

02

Demo class ke baad follow-up chhoot jata hai

Problem: Demo me 30 bachche aaye, admission 8 ne liya. Baaki 22 ko kisne dobara call kiya, kisi ko pata nahi. Yahi sabse bada admission leak hai.

autenflo: Demo ke 1, 3 aur 7 din baad parents ko alag-alag message jata hai. Jo parent reply kare, uska alert counsellor ko seedha.

03

Fees ke liye parents ko ek-ek call

Problem: Mahine ki 5 tarikh ke baad staff ghanto phone par lagta hai. Parents ko bura bhi lagta hai, aur kuch call uthate hi nahi.

autenflo: Due date se 3 din pehle tameez wala reminder, due ke din dobara, aur payment aate hi status update. Staff sirf unko call kare jo 7 din baad bhi baaki hain.

04

Absent bachche ka pata parents ko late chalta hai

Problem: Bachcha coaching ke naam par ghar se nikla aur class me gaya hi nahi. Parents ko hafte baad pata chalta hai, tab tak trust toot chuka hota hai.

autenflo: Attendance lagte hi absent bachche ke parents ko message. Hafte bhar me 3 absent hone par teacher ko alag alert.

05

Test result aur report card ka data entry

Problem: Har test ke baad marks excel me, phir parents ko ek-ek karke bhejna. Ek batch ka kaam 2 din le leta hai.

autenflo: Marks se report auto banti hai aur parents ko unke bachche ki hi report jati hai, topper aur weak topic ke saath.

06

Admission form aur documents ki typing

Problem: Form, marksheet, ID aur photo alag-alag aate hain. Kuch na kuch hamesha adhura rehta hai aur admission atak jata hai.

autenflo: Form se data auto entry, aur kaun sa document baaki hai uski checklist parents ko khud jati hai.

Mann me jo sawal hain

Coaching, Tuition aur Schools: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Coaching ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye parents ki enquiry ka turant jawab deta hai, demo class book karta hai, 1-3-7 din follow-up bhejta hai, fees ka reminder deta hai aur absent bachche ki khabar parents ko deta hai. Ye sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par hota hai.

Kya chhote tuition center ko bhi iski zaroorat hai?

Haan. Jahan roz 5 se 10 enquiry bhi aati hain, wahan ek bhi chhootne par admission jata hai. Chhote center me staff kam hota hai, isliye automation ka fayda aur zyada dikhta hai.

Admission season me ek saath 200 enquiry aaye to kya hoga?

AI ek saath kitni bhi chats sambhal leta hai. Har parent ko batch aur fees ka sahi jawab milta hai, aur jo parent seriously interested ho uska alert counsellor ko jata hai.

Kya AI parents se Hindi me baat karega?

Haan, Hindi me, aur zaroorat par English me. Bhasha aapke center ke andaaz ke hisaab se rakhi jati hai, taaki parents ko rojana jaisi baat lage.

Fees reminder se parents naraaz to nahi honge?

Reminder tameez se likha jata hai aur sirf due date ke aas-paas jata hai. Aap batate ho kitni baar aur kis tone me, aur payment aate hi reminder band ho jata hai.

Kya demo class ke baad follow-up khud hoga?

Haan. Demo ke 1, 3 aur 7 din baad alag message jata hai. Jo parent jawab de, wo hot lead banta hai aur counsellor ko turant alert milta hai.

School ke liye bhi ye kaam karega?

Haan. School me admission enquiry, fees reminder, PTM ki soochna, holiday notice aur attendance alert sabse zyada kaam aate hain. Teacher training ke liye alag AI training page bhi hai.

Setup me kitna time lagta hai aur kya office aana padega?

Setup online hota hai. Aap batch, fees aur timing batate ho, aur hum use AI me daalte hain. Aam taur par kuch hi din me chalu ho jata hai.

Parents ka data surakshit rahega?

Data aapke apne WhatsApp aur account me rehta hai aur sirf aapke kaam ke liye use hota hai. Hum privacy policy me likhe niyam follow karte hain.
