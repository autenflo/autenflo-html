Subah se party ke phone aate hain: aaj ka rate, maal kab niklega, mera hisaab kitna hai. Munim ji sab sambhalte hain, par jab kaam badhta hai to quotation late jati hai aur vasooli ki yaad chhoot jati hai.

Wholesale aur Hardware me kaam kahan atakta hai?

Wholesale ka paisa do jagah atakta hai: maal bechne se pehle quotation me, aur bechne ke baad vasooli me. Beech me order, challan aur stock ka hisaab diary, call aur chat me bikhra rehta hai. Yahan wahi 6 jagah hain jahan distributor aur trader ka time aur udhaar fansta hai.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Quotation manually banana

Problem: Har party ko alag rate, alag scheme aur alag quantity ka quotation banana padta hai. Late hua to party doosre supplier ko order de deti hai.

autenflo: Rate list se quotation turant banta hai aur party ko seedha chala jata hai. Party-wise special rate bhi yaad rehta hai.

02

Orders call, chat aur diary me bikhre

Problem: Ek order phone par, doosra WhatsApp par, teesra dukaan par aaya. Dispatch ke waqt koi na koi chhoot jata hai.

autenflo: Har order ek jagah darj hota hai aur confirmation party ko jata hai. Dispatch ki list roz apne aap ban jati hai.

03

Party-wise outstanding ginna, vasooli ke liye calls

Problem: Kis party par kitna baaki hai ye ginne me ghanta lagta hai, aur vasooli ke call karne me munim ji ka aadha din.

autenflo: Party-wise outstanding ka summary aur tameez wala payment reminder, due date ke hisaab se, apne aap.

04

Stock kam hone ka pata late chalta hai

Problem: Jab party ne maal manga tab pata chala ki godown me hai hi nahi. Order gaya aur party bhi gayi.

autenflo: Stock ek limit se neeche jate hi alert aata hai aur reorder ka reminder bhi.

05

Bill aur challan ki entry manually

Problem: Roz ke 40 bill aur challan ki typing me ek insaan lagta hai, aur galtiyan phir bhi hoti hain.

autenflo: Bill ya challan ki photo bhejte hi data apne aap entry ho jata hai.

06

Rate ya scheme badle to sabko ek-ek karke batana

Problem: Maal ka rate badla to 100 parties ko phone karna padta hai, aur jo reh gayi wo purane rate par order de deti hai.

autenflo: Rate ek baar update kiya aur sab parties ko naya rate ek saath chala gaya.

Mann me jo sawal hain

Wholesale aur Hardware: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

Wholesale ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye rate list se quotation banata hai, order record karta hai, outstanding par payment reminder bhejta hai aur stock kam hone par alert deta hai. Sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par chalta hai.

Kya ye chhote trader aur kirana distributor ke liye bhi hai?

Haan. Jahan 20 se 30 parties hain, wahan bhi rate, order aur vasooli ka kaam roz ka hota hai. Automation se munim ji ka time asli kaam me lagta hai.

Party-wise alag rate kaise yaad rahega?

Aap har party ka special rate ya discount ek baar daalte ho. Quotation banate waqt AI wahi rate use karta hai aur galti nahi hoti.

Vasooli ke reminder se party naraaz to nahi hogi?

Reminder tameez aur chhote andaaz me jata hai aur sirf due date ke aas-paas. Aap tay karte ho kitni baar aur kis party ko, aur payment aate hi band ho jata hai.

Kya bill ki photo se entry sach me ho jati hai?

Haan, bill ya challan ki saaf photo se naam, quantity aur amount nikal kar sheet me daal diya jata hai. Ek baar dekh lena achha rehta hai, par typing ka kaam khatam ho jata hai.

Stock ka alert kaise kaam karta hai?

Aap har maal ki minimum limit batate ho. Stock us limit se neeche jate hi aapko alert aur reorder ka reminder milta hai.

Kya naya rate sab parties ko ek saath bhej sakte hain?

Haan. Rate ek baar badlo aur chuni hui parties ko ek saath message chala jata hai. Jo party reply kare uska order turant record hota hai.

Kya ye Tally ya mere billing software ke saath chalega?

Aksar haan, kyunki ye WhatsApp aur aapki sheet ya software ke beech kaam karta hai. Pehle hum aapka process dekhte hain, phir batate hain kaise judega.

Hardware, steel ya kapde ke wholesale ke liye bhi chalega?

Haan. Har maal ke grade, rate aur minimum quantity alag daale ja sakte hain, aur sariya, cement ya kapde jaise maal ka roz badalta rate bhi ek baar update karke sabko mil jata hai.
