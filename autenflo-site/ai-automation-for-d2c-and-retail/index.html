D2C & Retail

D2C aur retail ke liye AI jo shaadi aur festival ki bheed me bhi har customer ko jawab deta hai.

Shaadi ka season hai. Instagram par 40 DM, WhatsApp par 30 message aur counter par 10 customer: "ye kitne ka hai", "COD hoga?", "parcel kahan hai?". Jis customer ko jawab pehle mila, order usi ka. Jise der se mila, wo agle seller ke paas chala gaya.

Ye page kis ke liye hai

D2C & Retail me kaam kahan atakta hai?

D2C brand ho ya garments, jewellery aur mobile ki dukaan, customer ke paas options bahut hain. Jo seller pehle price, size aur delivery batata hai, wahi order le jata hai. Enquiry, order, payment, COD aur parcel ka kaam alag-alag chats aur register me bikhra rehta hai. Neeche wahi 6 jagah hain jahan bikri aur time chhootte hain.

6 badi problems

Problem aur uska AI wala hal.

01

Photo bhejkar price poochne walon ko reply der se

Problem: Customer ne reel ya photo dekhkar poochha "ye kitne ka hai, size M hai?". Staff packing me laga tha, jawab aadhe ghante baad gaya. Tab tak customer kisi aur se khareed chuka tha.

autenflo: Catalogue, price, size aur delivery ka jawab turant jata hai. Jo customer serious lage, uska alert seedha aapko aata hai.

02

Order chat me, payment screenshot me, entry register me

Problem: Order WhatsApp par aata hai, payment ka screenshot alag chat me, aur entry register me. Din ke end me milana padta hai aur kuch na kuch chhoot jata hai.

autenflo: Order apne aap record hota hai, payment link ya UPI confirmation ka status update hota hai aur invoice customer ko chala jata hai.

03

COD order wapas aa jata hai, parcel kahan hai ke calls

Problem: Bina confirm kiye bheja gaya COD order aksar wapas aa jata hai. Upar se roz "mera parcel kahan hai" ke calls, jinka jawab dene me staff ka time jata hai.

autenflo: Dispatch se pehle customer se WhatsApp par address aur order confirm hota hai, aur parcel ka status customer ko apne aap milta hai. Wapas aane wale orders ka risk kam hota hai.

04

Purane customers wapas nahi aate

Problem: Jis customer ne pichhli shaadi ya Diwali me khareedari ki thi, usse dobara baat karne ka koi tareeka nahi. Wo bhool jata hai ki aapki dukaan ya brand hai.

autenflo: Festival, birthday aur anniversary par offer message khud jata hai. Jo cheez khatam hoti hai (jaise masala, makhana, sattu), uska reorder reminder bhi jata hai.

05

EMI, udhaar aur warranty ka reminder manually

Problem: Kis ki kist kab aani hai ya kis mobile ki service due hai, ye diary me likha hai. Reminder dena roz ka kaam hai aur kuch na kuch hamesha chhoot jata hai.

autenflo: Due date se pehle tameez ka reminder jata hai, payment aate hi status update hota hai, aur warranty ya service due par customer ko apne aap yaad dilaya jata hai.

06

Stock ka hisaab register me, content roz sochna

Problem: Kaun sa size ya model khatam hai, ye shaam ko register dekh kar pata chalta hai. Upar se roz sochna ki aaj kya post karein, aur page hafton tak purana dikhta hai.

autenflo: Stock aur sales apne aap update hote hain, low stock par alert aata hai. Naye collection par content calendar, Hindi captions aur auto-posting ka plan taiyar milta hai.

D2C & Retail: 10 sawal, 10 jawab

D2C aur retail ke liye AI WhatsApp automation kya karta hai?

Ye catalogue, price aur size turant bhejta hai, order aur payment ka record rakhta hai, COD confirm karta hai, parcel ka status batata hai, aur festival ya reorder offers chalata hai. Sab aapke hi WhatsApp number par hota hai.

Kya chhoti kapde, jewellery ya mobile ki dukaan ke liye bhi chalega?

Haan. Chhoti dukaan me staff kam hota hai, isliye counter ki bheed ke beech WhatsApp ka jawab sabse pehle chhootta hai. Automation yahi kaam sambhal leta hai.

Mera business Instagram par chalta hai, kya wo bhi judega?

Aksar haan. Instagram ya Facebook se aane wale customer ko WhatsApp par laakar wahan jawab, order aur follow-up automate hota hai. Kya-kya judega, ye free audit me aapke setup dekhkar bata dete hain.

COD ke fake ya wapas aane wale orders ka kya hoga?

Dispatch se pehle customer se address aur order WhatsApp par confirm karwaya jata hai. Isse wapas aane wale orders ka risk kam hota hai, par poori guarantee koi nahi de sakta.

Jewellery me rate roz badalta hai, uska kya?

Aap aaj ka rate ek baar update karte ho aur AI usi se jawab deta hai. Purana rate kisi customer ko nahi jata.

Customer photo bheje to AI kya karega?

Wo customer ko milta-julta design aur price dikhata hai, aur jahan sahi lage wahan aapko alert deta hai. Aap batate ho kya khud sambhalna hai.

Kya EMI aur udhaar ke reminder se customer naraaz honge?

Reminder tameez se aur due date ke aas-paas jata hai. Payment aate hi reminder band ho jata hai, aur aap tay karte ho kitni baar jaye.

Kya ye mere billing software ke saath chalega?

Aksar haan, kyunki ye WhatsApp aur aapki sheet ya software ke beech kaam karta hai. Pehle hum aapka process dekhte hain, phir batate hain kaise judega.

Purane customer ka data kaise rakha jata hai?

Naam, number aur purani khareed aapke apne account me rehti hai aur sirf aapke kaam ke liye use hoti hai. Hum privacy policy me likhe niyam follow karte hain.
